Rebecca

Inspect CAD models with your hands.

Rebecca watches your webcam and turns a pinch into orbit, a fist into pan and two hands into zoom. Press a hotkey, gesture at the model, press it again.

Made for looking, not modeling

Rebecca is for inspecting a finished model, or a link someone just sent you. Keep the mouse for sketching and use your hands to turn the part around.

  • Pinch and moveOrbit around the part
  • Make a fist and movePan the view
  • Pinch with both hands, spread or closeZoom in and out
  • Two fists, turn like a wheelTwist (rolls the view in SolidWorks)
  • Open your handPause, like lifting a mouse to reposition

These colors match the indicator that appears at the top of your screen while Rebecca is on.

  1. Install and pick your camera. Rebecca runs in the system tray. Right-click its icon, open Camera and choose your webcam.
  2. Put your cursor over the model and press Ctrl+Alt+R. The camera turns on and you hear a short beep.
  3. Gesture, then press the hotkey again. The camera turns off. It also turns off by itself after 45 seconds without hands.

Works with the viewer you're in

Rebecca reads the title of the window you're looking at and picks the right mouse mapping, so a browser tab showing Onshape behaves like Onshape.

  • Fusion 360
  • Onshape
  • SolidWorks
  • Blender
  • FreeCAD
  • Sketchfab and Printables viewers

Questions

Does Rebecca record or upload my video?

No. Video is processed on your computer and never saved or sent anywhere. The camera is on only while a session is running, and Rebecca has no accounts or analytics. It contacts the internet only if you click "Check for updates".

What do I need?

A webcam and Windows 10 or 11. Good, even light on your hands helps more than a better camera.

How do I change the hotkey, speed or camera?

Right-click the tray icon. Hotkey, camera, speed, zoom direction, the on-screen indicator, sounds and start-at-login are all in the menu.

The camera won't open

Another app, such as Zoom, Teams or a browser tab, is probably using it. Close that app, or pick a different camera from the tray menu.

Can I try it without installing?

Yes. The browser version runs the same gestures on a sample part. Your video stays in the tab.

Why "Rebecca"?

It stands for Really Exciting Borderline Ecstatic CAD Control Articulator.
